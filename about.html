About Kingsman Events


Kingsman Events is a professional event planning and management company dedicated to creating
memorable experiences for individuals, businesses, and organisations. Our goal is to deliver
well-organised, high-quality events that meet each client’s vision, objectives, and budget.
Whether planning private celebrations, sporting events, corporate functions, or community
programmes, we focus on creativity, precision, and professionalism at every stage.


At Kingsman Events, we believe that every event should be more than just an occasion —
it should be an experience. From the earliest planning stages to the final moments of the event,
our team works closely with clients to ensure every detail is carefully planned and flawlessly executed.

Our Event Planning Process

Pre-Event Planning
  • Defining event goals and objectives
  • Organising roles and budgets
  • Venue selection
  • Event structure planning and logistics
  • Planning promotion strategies
  • Risk management

During the Event
  • On-site operations and coordination
  • Monitoring event schedules and activities
  • Managing staff, vendors, and performers
  • Ensuring a smooth and engaging experience for guests

Post-Event Evaluation
  • Event closure and breakdown
  • Collecting feedback from clients and attendees
  • Analysing success data
  • Clean up and equipment removal
  • Reporting and follow-up

Our Services


Kingsman Events provides event planning and management services for individuals, businesses,
and organisations. Once a client presents a project brief, our team carefully assesses the
type, scale, and purpose of the event and develops a structured planning strategy.


Throughout the planning and execution stages, we maintain regular communication with our
clients to ensure all requirements are clearly defined and successfully delivered.
Our services include coordinating catering, equipment, venues, logistics, and event personnel.
By managing all operational aspects, we ensure efficient delivery and a smooth,
professional experience.

Charity and Community Engagement


Kingsman Events places strong emphasis on charity events and community programmes as part of
our commitment to social responsibility. We organise charity fundraising events that raise
financial support for orphanages, churches, and underfunded care facilities while also
increasing awareness of important social issues.


In addition to fundraising initiatives, we develop community programmes focused on supporting
disadvantaged individuals and families. These programmes collect donations such as
non-perishable food items and essential supplies for those in need.


Looking ahead, Kingsman Events aims to establish soup kitchens in key locations across Scotland.
These facilities will provide hot meals for homeless and vulnerable individuals while working
closely with local councils, charities, churches, and volunteers to support community wellbeing.

Our Mission


Our mission is to create exceptional events while making a positive impact in the communities
we serve.
